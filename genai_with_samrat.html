A little about us

Technology moves fast. Learning can feel human.

GenAI With Samrat is a learning space for people who want to understand emerging technology and put it to work. We make room for questions, practical experimentation, and progress that builds one step at a time.

Whether you're just meeting generative AI or ready to explore more advanced applications, the goal stays the same: make complex ideas approachable and help you keep learning with purpose.

Our learning philosophy

A simple path from “what if?” to “I made this.”

You don't need to know everything to begin. You just need a clear next step and room to experiment.
